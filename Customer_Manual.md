# Fleetly Pro - Customer User Manual

Welcome to Fleetly Pro, your premium GPS tracking and fleet management solution. This guide will walk you through the core features of your new dashboard.

## 1. Accessing Your Dashboard

To access your vehicles:
1. Navigate to the login page (e.g., `http://your-domain.com`).
2. Enter your secure **Username** and **Password** provided by your administrator.
3. Click **Access Dashboard**.

If your subscription has expired, you will see a lock screen. Please contact your Admin to recharge your account.

---

## 2. The Interactive Map

When you log in, you will be greeted by the live tracking map.
- **Filter Bar (Bottom):** Quickly filter your view between **ALL** vehicles, **ACTIVE** (currently driving), and **IDLE** (parked/stopped).
- **Map Layers (Top Right):** Click the layer icon to toggle between:
  - **Standard View**
  - **Dark Mode** (High contrast, ideal for night monitoring)
  - **Satellite View**

---

## 3. Vehicle 360 Details Panel

To see in-depth information about a specific vehicle, simply click its card in the left sidebar, or click its marker directly on the map. 

A sleek panel will slide out from the right, displaying:
- **Live Speedometer:** A gauge that animates in real-time as your vehicle's speed changes.
- **Odometer:** Total distance traveled by the vehicle.
- **Ignition Status:** Whether the engine is currently ON or OFF.
- **Internal Battery:** The percentage of backup power remaining in the tracker itself (e.g., if the main vehicle battery is disconnected).
- **GPS Lock:** Displays the quality of the satellite connection (e.g., "3D Fix") and the number of satellites connected.
- **Live Coordinates:** The exact Latitude and Longitude of the vehicle.

---

## 4. Adding a New Tracker

If you have purchased a new hardware tracker, you must link it to your account before it appears on your map.

1. Click the **+ Add Tracker** button in the left sidebar.
2. Enter the **15-digit IMEI number** printed on the back of your physical tracker.
3. Click **Link Tracker**.
4. This sends a request to your Administrator. Once approved, the vehicle will instantly appear on your dashboard!

---

## 5. Understanding Telemetry Metrics

- **3D Fix:** This means your tracker is connected to 4 or more satellites in space, providing a highly accurate location. 
- **No Fix:** The tracker cannot see the sky (e.g., parked in an underground garage) and is using its last known location.
- **Battery Percentage:** This shows the backup battery of the tracker. If it drops below 20%, the icon will turn red, indicating that the main vehicle battery may have been disconnected or is failing to charge the device.
